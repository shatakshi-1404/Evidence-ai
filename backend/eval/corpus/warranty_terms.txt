Meridian Home Warranty Terms

All Meridian thermostats include a limited warranty of 3 years from the date of purchase. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use.

The warranty does not cover damage caused by incorrect wiring, power surges, unauthorized repairs, water exposure or modification of the device. Batteries and cosmetic wear are also excluded.

To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a short description of the fault. Our team will usually ask for a photo of the wiring behind the device. Approved claims are resolved by replacement with the same or an equivalent model. We do not offer cash refunds for warranty claims.

Extended coverage is available through the Meridian Care plan, which adds 2 more years and includes accidental damage protection. Meridian Care must be purchased within 60 days of buying the thermostat and costs 39 dollars.

Replacement units carry only the remaining period of the original warranty. Warranty coverage cannot be transferred when a thermostat is resold, but it does transfer when the device is installed in a home that is sold.
