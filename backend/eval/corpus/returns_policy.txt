Meridian Home Returns Policy

Customers may return any unused thermostat within 45 days of delivery for a full refund. The thermostat must be in its original packaging with all cables, mounting plates and the quick start guide included.

To start a return, sign in to your Meridian account and open the Orders page. Choose the order, select Return item, and print the prepaid shipping label. Returns without a return authorization number will not be accepted at our warehouse.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned item is inspected. Shipping fees are refundable only if the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong model. Gift purchases are refunded as store credit unless the buyer provides the original receipt.

Opened accessories such as temperature sensors and wall plates can be returned within 14 days. Software licenses and subscription plans are not returnable once activated, but the Meridian Plus subscription can be cancelled at any time and will not renew.

A restocking fee of 15 percent applies to bulk orders of ten or more thermostats that are returned after 30 days. Business customers should contact the commercial support team before returning a bulk order.
