Meridian Home Employee Leave Policy

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid vacation per month, which is 18 days per year. Vacation accrual begins on the first day of employment, and employees may use accrued days after completing 90 days of service.

Unused vacation days may be carried over into the next year up to a maximum of 5 days. Any days above that limit expire on December 31. Vacation requests of more than 5 consecutive days should be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance.

Employees receive 10 paid sick days each calendar year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick days do not carry over from one year to the next.

New parents are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave, taken within the first 12 months after birth or adoption. Parental leave can be taken in one block or split into two periods with manager approval.

Employees may take up to 3 days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member. Additional unpaid time off can be arranged with human resources.

Meridian observes 9 company holidays each year, including the day after Thanksgiving. The holiday calendar is published each November for the following year.
