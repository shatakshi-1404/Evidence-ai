Meridian Home Shipping Guide

Standard shipping within the continental United States takes 3 to 6 business days and is free for orders over 75 dollars. Orders under 75 dollars pay a flat shipping fee of 6.95 dollars.

Express shipping delivers within 2 business days and costs 18 dollars. Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern time ship the same day. Orders placed after the cutoff ship the next business day.

We ship to Canada and Mexico with delivery in 7 to 12 business days. International customers are responsible for import duties and taxes, which are collected by the carrier at delivery. We do not currently ship to addresses outside North America.

Every shipment includes a tracking number sent by email once the package leaves our warehouse in Reno, Nevada. If a package shows as delivered but has not arrived, wait 24 hours and check with neighbors before contacting support, as carriers often scan packages early.

Lost packages are replaced at no cost once the carrier confirms the loss, which can take up to 10 business days. Signature confirmation is available on request for orders over 300 dollars.
